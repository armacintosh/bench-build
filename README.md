## ✨ Three Signature Design Features

1. **Sunken 1.00" Recessed Cushion Pocket (`Z = 19.75"` to `20.75"`)**
   A `3/4"` Birch Plywood seat deck (`78.50" × 27.50"`) sits atop a coplanar `2x4` torsion-box grid (`Z = 19.00"..19.75"`), surrounded by a mitered `1x6` stain-grade outer apron rising to `Z = 20.75"`. That creates a **1.00" perimeter lip** that locks a standard `78" × 24" × 4"` foam bench cushion in place without Velcro.
2. **"Wheelbarrow" Hidden Mobility (`3/8"` Ground Clearance)**
   The **2 front corner legs** rest on high-friction `3/8"` solid rubber pads (100% locked when kids jump or climb). The **3 rear legs** house recessed fixed-axle rubber rollers inside a `4.00" × 1.50"` bottom notch so the wheels protrude just `3/8"`. Lift the front apron by 1 inch to roll the entire couch effortlessly across the room.
3. **Drop-In 10° Slanted Backrests (2 Independent 40" Modules)**
   Four captive wooden mortise sleeves behind the main back rail accept the `4.25"` bottom tenons of the `2x6` angled backrest uprights (`10°` ergonomic recline). Lift either module straight up in seconds to convert half or all of the couch into a flat play table.

## 🔨 7-Step Assembly Overview

![Step 1 and Step 3 3D CAD Visuals](assets/images/step3_cross_boards_3d_cad.jpg)

| Step | Phase Name | Key Action Summary | Diagram |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **1** | **Prep the 5 `4x4` Legs** | Cut 5 posts (`A1`–`A5`) to `18-5/8"`. Notch a `4.0" × 1.5"` bottom wheel slot in the 3 rear legs (`A3`, `A4`, `A5`) and drill a `5/16"` axle hole `1-3/8"` up from the bottom. | [View SVG](assets/blueprints/phase_01_legs_and_wheel_notches.svg) |
| **2** | **Outer Frame & 4 Backrest Sleeves** | Glue & screw the `71.5"` inner front beam (`B1`) + `78.5"` outer front beam (`B2`), attach the `71.5"` main back rail (`B3`), and build the 4 rear `1.5" × 3.5"` mortise sleeves (`D1`–`D5` + `B4`). | [View SVG](assets/blueprints/phase_02_outer_frame_and_sleeves.svg) |
| **3** | **5 Cross Ribs & Rear-Center Leg** | Install the 5 `20.75"` front-to-back `2x4` cross ribs (`C1`–`C5`) flush at `Z = 19.00"` and mount the rear-center post (`A5`) under the middle rib (`C3`). | [View SVG](assets/blueprints/phase_03_interior_ribs_and_center_leg.svg) |
| **4** | **`3/4"` Birch Plywood Seat Deck** | Glue and screw the `78.50" × 27.50"` plywood deck (`E1`) atop the `2x4` grid using `#8 × 1-3/4"` screws every `8"`. | [View SVG](assets/blueprints/phase_04_plywood_seat_deck.svg) |
| **5** | **Mitered `1x6` Trim Apron** | Wrap the frame with `45°`-mitered `1x6` front/back rails (`80.0"`) and side stiles (`29.0"`), rising `1.00"` above the plywood deck (`Z = 20.75"`). | [View SVG](assets/blueprints/phase_05_mitered_1x6_trim_apron.svg) |
| **6** | **Drop-In `10°` Backrest Modules** | Cut four `2x6` uprights (`G1`–`G4`) with `4.25"` bottom tenons and `10°` upper faces; screw three `39-7/8"` `1x4` slats (`H1`–`H3` / `H4`–`H6`) onto each pair. | [View SVG](assets/blueprints/phase_06_slanted_backrest_modules.svg) |
| **7** | **Sand, Finish, Wheels & Cushions** | Round over edges (`1/8"` radius), apply water-based polyurethane, attach the 2 front `3/8"` rubber pads + 3 rear axle rollers, and drop in the cushions. | [View SVG](assets/blueprints/phase_07_finishing_pads_and_rollers.svg) |
